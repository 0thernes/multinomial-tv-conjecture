# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this scientific research repository a harassment-free, rigorous, and constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, affiliation, or level of experience.

## Scientific & Community Standards

- **Critique arguments, definitions, and code—never people.** Hostile counterexamples, fidelity audits, and falsification reports are explicitly welcomed and rewarded with credit in `ERRATA.md` and `REPLICATION.md`.
- **Be honest about epistemic boundaries.** Clearly separate kernel-checked facts, exact rational computations, floating-point checks, and unverified conjectures.
- **Zero harassment, personal attacks, or misrepresentation.**

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior or concerns privately to Alexander S. Donahue at `0_0@0thernes.art`. Adapted from Contributor Covenant v2.1 (https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
