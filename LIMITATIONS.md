# Limitations, Verification Boundaries & Real-vs-Metaphor (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document states explicitly what our Lean 4 formalization and numerical verification **do** establish, what they **do not** establish, and where literal mathematical claims stop.

## 2. Specific Technical & Process Limitations

1. **No external peer review yet:** The manuscript is an independent research preprint. It has NOT yet undergone journal peer review, arXiv moderation, or Palomar Registry acceptance. The source of the conjecture, `arXiv:2610.17046v1`, is itself an arXiv v1 preprint (posted 2026-10-12) with no journal reference.
2. **Human line-by-line fidelity audit & external operator replication are open:** `Challenge.lean` is 164 lines of Mathlib-only Lean 4 (with a Lean 4.35 `module` header, 10 targets). `NonVacuity.lean` proves 22 small-$L$, small-$n$, and adversarial checks: **6** of them (`hAdj 0..5`) test the definition `hAdj` directly, and the other **16** are corollaries/refutations of the proved closed forms covering all 10 targets (including `adversarial_lemma_7_3_zero_const_false` and `gamma3_source_form_witness_N1`, so they verify non-degeneracy and witness coverage but cannot by themselves detect a wrong specification). No independent third-party mathematician has yet signed off line-by-line on `docs/statement-fidelity.md` or logged an external clean-room replication (`B44: partial` in `repo.yaml`).
3. **Lean 4.35.0 `module` system & Palomar readiness:** All 10 Lean source files carry Lean 4.35.0 `module` headers (`public import` + `@[expose] public section`) and compile with **zero `@[nolint]` suppressions** and no compiler or Mathlib style-linter warnings except the 10 intentional `declaration uses sorry` warnings of the statement-only `Challenge.lean` (`weak.linter.mathlibStandardSet = true`), satisfying Palomar Registry's file-level `module` and size rules (`Challenge.lean` 164 lines / ~7.8 KiB, longest file 3,572 lines).
4. **Windows vs. Linux sandbox for `lake comparator`:** On Linux (`ubuntu-24.04` CI), `lake comparator --config comparator.json --paranoid` makes all six checker runs (`Lean paranoid`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`, `Lean default`) inside a `bubblewrap` namespace sandbox. Upstream Lean 4.35.0 `lake comparator` requires Linux `bubblewrap` and does not run directly on native Windows; on Windows, 5 of the 6 bundled checkers (`lean 4.35.0`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `nanoda_bin`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`) can be invoked directly on `leanexport` output while the full comparator run (including `lean4lean`) needs Linux containers or Ubuntu 24.04 CI.
5. **AI-assisted provenance:** Mathematical exploration, Lean 4 proof engineering, Python test scaffolding, and LaTeX drafting were conducted with AI assistance under Alexander S. Donahue's direction (see `formalization.yaml` and the paragraph *"AI assistance, CRediT authorship, conflicts of interest, and errata policy"* in `paper/main.tex`). Epistemic warrant rests strictly on the reproducible Lean kernel check, the CI comparator replay, and the independent exact calculations, never on author or model authority.
6. **Historical 2026-11-26 statement audit note:** In the external statement audit of commit `0a69443` (`v2.3.0`), `lake exe cache get` failed on the auditor's machine (local Mathlib cache error), so that audit relied on CI run `45832736228` and file-by-file elaboration rather than a local comparator run. From `v2.5.0` onward, the full Lean build and all six comparator checker runs execute cleanly on public GitHub Actions CI.
7. **Elaboration smoke test & negative comparator test vs. kernel-checked refutation:** The mathematical refutations of $a_\triangle = 1/32$ and of a zero-constant $O(L^{-2})$ remainder are the proved theorems `adversarial_aTriangle_ne_one_div_32` and `adversarial_lemma_7_3_zero_const_false` in `NonVacuity.lean`. Separately, CI runs (a) a standalone elaboration smoke test (`/tmp/neg_smoke_test.lean` importing `MultinomialTVConjecture.Main` and asserting `unsolved goals` on `aTriangle = 1 / 32`), and (b) a live negative `lake comparator --paranoid` tamper test (injecting `sorry` into a subshell-guarded copy of `Solution.lean` Target 4 and asserting non-zero rejection). Earlier versions (`v2.1.0`–`v2.3.0`) overclaimed the smoke test as a standalone proof of falsity (corrected in `v2.5.0`), and `v2.5.0`–`v2.6.0` inaccurately described it as copying `Main.lean` (`F-74`, fixed in `v2.7.0`).
8. **Export-control self-screen (EAR 15 CFR 734.8 / ITAR 22 CFR 120):** This repository contains solely published fundamental mathematical research in probability and statistics (asymptotic variance of discrete total variation under the uniform multinomial law) with zero cryptographic, defense, or dual-use technical data or software.

## 3. Real vs. Metaphor

- Every mathematical term in this repository (`multinomial distribution`, `discrete total variation`, `Euler–Maclaurin summation`, `Stirling expansion`, `asymptotic variance`, `residue class`) is used strictly in its **literal, standard mathematical definition**—there are no physical, biological, or heuristic metaphors.
- The axiom-audit block in `README.md` is an excerpt (25 of the 59 `depends on axioms` lines and the summary line). Re-running `lake env lean VerifyAxioms.lean` against pre-built `.olean` caches prints the full output; a cold build without Mathlib's `lake exe cache get` takes substantially longer (~2–5 minutes with cache, >30 minutes compiling Mathlib from source).
- **"Six checker runs"** means the six passes of `lake comparator --paranoid` over `Challenge.lean` and `Solution.lean`: the Lean 4.35.0 kernel in paranoid and default mode, `lean4lean`, `nanoda 0.4.19`, `con-leche`, and `con-ron`. Two of the six are the Lean kernel, and whether `con-leche` and `con-ron` are separate implementations has not been checked here. The replay does **not** remove the need to inspect `Challenge.lean` itself for statement fidelity against Banić & Elezović (2026).
