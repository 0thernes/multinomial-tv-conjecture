# Limitations, Verification Boundaries & Real-vs-Metaphor (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

This document states explicitly what our Lean 4 formalization and numerical verification **do** establish, what they **do not** establish, and where literal mathematical claims stop.

## 1. Formal Verification Boundary (What Lean Checks vs. What Lies Outside)

| Layer | Inside Lean 4 (kernel-checked) | Outside Lean 4 (Cited / Scripted / Human-Audited) |
|---|---|---|
| **Combinatorial definition & reduction** | `hAdj L` equals exact nested multinomial sum (`hAdj_eq_nested_sum`); exact 1D identities for `L = 3q, 3q+1, 3q+2`. | Equivalence between the discrete sum and probabilistic measure-space notation `E[|X_2 - X_1||X_3 - X_2|]` is standard finite-support probability (audited in [docs/statement-fidelity.md](docs/statement-fidelity.md)). |
| **Conjecture 7.7 (`b_0, b_1, b_2, d_0, a_△, γ_2`)** | Kernel-checked by Lean 4.35.0 from Mathlib, using only the three standard axioms. The targets are replayed in CI by six comparator checker runs (the Lean kernel in paranoid and default mode, `lean4lean`, `nanoda`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`). Lean proves the three limits `b_r`, not the $O(\ell^{-2})$ remainder that Part 2 Lemma 7.3 asserts. | Independent human line-by-line fidelity review of `Challenge.lean` against `arXiv:2610.17046v1` is **OPEN** (`human_line_by_line_review_recorded: false` in `formalization.yaml`). The source is an unrefereed arXiv v1 preprint. |
| **Linear, cyclic & small-bin formulas `γ(n)`, `γ_c(n)`, `γ(3;N)`, `γ(4;N)`** | Exact algebraic/trigonometric identities after substituting `aTriangle` and `bRes` (`gamma_closed_form`, `gamma_cyclic_closed_form`, `gamma3_closed_form`, `gamma4_closed_form`). Lean defines no random variable or variance; `n` is any nonzero real, while the source treats integer `n ≥ 5` and `n = 3, 4` separately. In the `n = 4` identity the term `(−1)^N/(2π)` is carried on both sides, not derived. | That these expressions are the `N^{-2}` variance coefficients is Theorem 7.6, Lemmas 7.2, 7.3, 7.5, §7.2 and §8 of Banić & Elezović Part 2 (`arXiv:2610.17046v1`) and Theorem 3.10 of Part 5 (`arXiv:2611.14735v1`): **cited external results from unrefereed preprints, NOT proved in Lean**. Lemma 7.5 uses the remainder bound of Lemma 7.3, which is not reproved here. |
| **Numerical verification (`scripts/verify_conjecture.py`)** | Exact rational `hAdj 0..5` proved in `NonVacuity.lean`. | Exact rational checks for $L=3..45$ (43 values) and 80-digit `mpmath` checks up to $q=3{,}000$ ($L=9{,}001$; 28-point Neville–Richardson error $< 3\times 10^{-55}$) run in Python (`fractions` + `mpmath`), outside the Lean kernel. |
| **Manuscript & prose (`paper/main.tex`)** | Theorem statements correspond 1-to-1 to Lean declarations (`docs/statement-fidelity.md`, `blueprint/src/content.tex`). | LaTeX typesetting, historical context, and prose exposition are human/AI-authored and NOT checked by a proof assistant. The frozen `v2.3.0` manuscript contains wrong Part 5 locators and overstates the Lean scope of Corollary 1.2; see [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md). |

## 2. Specific Technical & Process Limitations

1. **No external peer review yet:** The manuscript is an independent research preprint. It has NOT yet undergone journal peer review, arXiv moderation, or Palomar Registry acceptance. The source of the conjecture, `arXiv:2610.17046v1`, is itself an arXiv v1 preprint (posted 2026-10-12) with no journal reference.
2. **Human line-by-line fidelity audit is open:** `Challenge.lean` is only 143 lines of Mathlib-only Lean 4 (with a Lean 4.35 `module` header, 8 targets). `NonVacuity.lean` proves 20 small-$L$, small-$n$ and adversarial checks; 6 of them (`hAdj 0..5`) test the definition `hAdj` directly, and the other 14 are corollaries of the proved closed forms, so they cannot detect a wrong definition. No independent third-party mathematician has yet signed off line-by-line on `docs/statement-fidelity.md`.
3. **Lean 4.35.0 `module` system & Palomar readiness:** As of `v2.3.0`, all 10 Lean source files carry Lean 4.35.0 `module` headers (`public import` + `@[expose] public section`) and compile with no compiler or Mathlib style-linter warnings except the 8 intentional `declaration uses sorry` warnings of the statement-only `Challenge.lean` (`weak.linter.mathlibStandardSet = true`), satisfying Palomar Registry's file-level `module` and size rules (`Challenge.lean` 143 lines / ~6.5 KiB, longest file 2,025 lines).
4. **Windows vs. Linux sandbox for `lake comparator`:** On Linux (`ubuntu-24.04` CI), `lake comparator --config comparator.json --paranoid` makes all six checker runs (`Lean paranoid`, `lean4lean`, `nanoda`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`, `Lean default`) inside a `bubblewrap` namespace sandbox. Upstream Lean 4.35.0 `lake comparator` requires Linux `bubblewrap` and does not run directly on native Windows; on Windows, 5 of the 6 bundled checkers (`lean 4.35.0`, `leanchecker-paranoid`, `nanoda_bin`, `con-leche`, `con-ron`) can be invoked directly on `leanexport` output while the full comparator run (including `lean4lean`) needs Linux containers or Ubuntu 24.04 CI.
5. **AI-assisted provenance:** Mathematical exploration, Lean 4 proof engineering, Python test scaffolding, and LaTeX drafting were conducted with AI assistance under Alexander S. Donahue's direction (see `formalization.yaml` and the paragraph *"AI assistance, CRediT authorship, conflicts of interest, and errata policy"* in `paper/main.tex`). Epistemic warrant rests on the reproducible Lean kernel check, the CI comparator replay and the independent exact calculations, never on author or model authority.
6. **Not rebuilt in the 2026-11-26 statement audit:** In that audit `lake exe cache get` failed on the auditor's machine (local Mathlib cache error), so no `lake build` completed there. The kernel-check evidence for that audit is CI run `45832736228` at commit `0a69443` and a file-by-file elaboration of all ten Lean files against prebuilt Mathlib. The comparator was not run in that audit.
7. **Negative CI step:** The CI step in `.github/workflows/lean-verify.yml` described in earlier versions as a "live negative kernel rejection check" supplies a proof of a different statement and passes on any error. It does not test whether `aTriangle = 1/32` is false. That refutation is the proved theorem `adversarial_aTriangle_ne_one_div_32` in `NonVacuity.lean`.
8. **Export-control self-screen (EAR 15 CFR 734.8 / ITAR 22 CFR 120):** This repository contains solely published fundamental mathematical research in probability and statistics (asymptotic variance of discrete total variation under the uniform multinomial law) with zero cryptographic, defense, or dual-use technical data or software.

## 3. Real vs. Metaphor

- Every mathematical term in this repository (`multinomial distribution`, `discrete total variation`, `Euler–Maclaurin summation`, `Stirling expansion`, `asymptotic variance`, `residue class`) is used strictly in its **literal, standard mathematical definition**—there are no physical, biological, or heuristic metaphors.
- The axiom-audit block in `README.md` is an excerpt (21 of the 49 `depends on axioms` lines and the summary line). Re-running `lake env lean VerifyAxioms.lean` against pre-built `.olean` caches prints the full output; a cold build without Mathlib's `lake exe cache get` takes substantially longer (~2–5 minutes with cache, >30 minutes compiling Mathlib from source).
- **"Six checker runs"** means the six passes of `lake comparator --paranoid` over `Challenge.lean` and `Solution.lean`: the Lean 4.35.0 kernel in paranoid and default mode, `lean4lean`, `nanoda 0.4.19`, `con-leche` and `con-ron`. Two of the six are the Lean kernel, and whether `con-leche` and `con-ron` are separate implementations has not been checked here. The replay does **not** remove the need to inspect `Challenge.lean` itself for statement fidelity against Banić & Elezović (2026).
