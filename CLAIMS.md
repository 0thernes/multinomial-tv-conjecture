# Claims Ledger (`CLAIMS.md`)

Every mathematical and computational claim in this repository is numbered, assigned an exact verification tier, mapped to its Lean 4 declaration / script / test, and paired with an explicit falsification criterion (**what would prove us wrong**).

**Source status.** Conjecture 7.7 is stated in the arXiv preprint `arXiv:2610.17046v1` of N. Banić and N. Elezović (posted 2026-10-12, v1 only, no journal reference; not peer reviewed). Part 5 of the same series (`arXiv:2611.14735v1`, Remark 3.11) still lists the closed form as unproved. The proof in this repository is also not peer reviewed.

**Tiers.** "Kernel-checked" means the Lean 4.35.0 kernel accepted the proof. The 8 targets of `Challenge.lean` and the declarations they depend on are also replayed in CI by `lake comparator --paranoid`, which makes six checker runs: the Lean kernel in paranoid and default mode, `lean4lean`, `nanoda`, `con-leche` and `con-ron`. Two of the six runs are the Lean kernel.

## Explicit Falsification Protocol (What Would Prove Us Wrong)

To refute our core mathematical result (**C3**), a critic only needs to produce **any one** of the following:
1. **A definition-fidelity counterexample:** Show that `Challenge.hAdj`, `Challenge.cL`, `Challenge.aStar`, `Challenge.bRes`, `Challenge.aTriangle`, or `Challenge.gamma2` in [lean/Challenge.lean](lean/Challenge.lean) fails to match the literal mathematical definitions in Banić & Elezović (`arXiv:2610.17046v1`, Section 7.2).
2. **A kernel soundness breach:** Demonstrate that `lean/VerifyAxioms.lean` or `lake comparator --config comparator.json --paranoid` fails on a clean install of Lean 4.35.0, or depends on any axiom outside `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`.
3. **A numerical contradiction:** Produce a high-precision exact evaluation of $h_{\mathrm{adj}}(L)$ at large $L$ whose asymptotic $1/L$ residue limits disagree with $b_0 = \frac{32\sqrt{3}}{81\pi}$ or $b_1 = b_2 = -\frac{22\sqrt{3}}{81\pi}$ beyond the $O(L^{-2})$ error bound.
4. **An upstream retraction:** If Banić & Elezović retract or amend Theorem 7.6 (Part 2) or Theorem 3.10 (Part 5), Claim **C4** would need re-derivation (while Claims **C1–C3** regarding $h_{\mathrm{adj}}(L)$, $b_r$, $d_0$, $a_\triangle$, and $\gamma_2$ would remain mathematically valid standalone theorems).

Any verified refutation will be credited prominently in [ERRATA.md](ERRATA.md) and [REPLICATION.md](REPLICATION.md).
